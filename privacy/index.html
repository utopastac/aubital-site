Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Aubital (“Aubital”, “we”, “us”) is a drawing instrument for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app handles and what it does not.

Summary

Aubital does not require an account, does not sell personal data, and does not include advertising or third-party analytics. Creations and settings stay on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your drawings or audio.

Information on your device

  • Creations. Loops, instrument choices, and library entries are stored in the app’s documents on your device.
  • Settings. Preferences such as rate and appearance stay on your device so they are still there the next time you open the app.
  • Exports. Sharing opens the system share sheet with a still of the current view. The picture leaves the app only if you choose to save or send it.

What we do not collect

  • No account registration or login.
  • No contact information, location, or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs from us.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No cloud library operated by Aubital.

Apple and the operating system

Apple may collect diagnostic or usage information according to your device settings and Apple’s own privacy policy, for example if you have opted in to Share iPhone Analytics or App Store diagnostics. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by Aubital.

Children

Aubital does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of Aubital after a change means you accept the updated policy.
