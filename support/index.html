Aubital needs iOS 26 on iPhone or iPad. Creations are stored on the device. Deleting the app deletes that library.

Draw

Drag on the cylinder to place notes. Height is pitch; the spin sets the beat. Lift to finish a stroke. Stack loops, undo, or clear from the editor chrome.

Instruments and rate

Open Instruments to choose a voice and try sounds on the pad. Rate sets how many pulses fit in one turn of the cylinder.

Free look and export

Free look lets you orbit the sculpture. Export opens the system share sheet with a still of the current view.

Where are creations stored?

The library lives in the app’s documents on your device. Aubital does not upload creations. Export anything you want to keep before you delete the app.
